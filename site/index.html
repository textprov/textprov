An open protocol for in-band provenance

Text that remembers where it came from.

TextProv marks whether text was written by a person, generated by a machine, or has an unknown origin. The provenance travels as Unicode, surviving copy, paste, and plain-text storage.

How it works

A small contract,
a durable signal.

  1. 1

    Mark

    A producer adds one provenance selector after each non-whitespace grapheme cluster.

    textprov.mark("Hello", state="ai")
  2. 2

    Move

    The text passes through the tools people already use. No side-channel has to follow it.

    copy → paste → store
  3. 3

    Render

    A decoder exposes marked runs to the web, an editor, or a provenance-aware font.

    textprov.render(document.body)

Use it today

No font. No framework. Two files.

The browser decorator turns in-band marks into semantic spans with a stable data-prov attribute. Bring your own visual language or use the reference stylesheet.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="textprov.css">
<script src="textprov.js"></script>

<script>
  textprov.render(document.body);
</script>
