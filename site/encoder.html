TextProv encoder

Add a declared origin to text, then copy or download the marked result. TextProv stores the label as normally invisible Unicode characters within the text.

A label is a claim, not proof. This tool does not detect AI writing or verify authorship.

Encode text

Choose a label and enter text on the left. The marked UTF-8 result appears on the right. Processing happens in your browser; your text is not sent to a server.

Declared origin

The embedded marks are normally invisible, so the two text areas may look identical. Copy or download the result without changing its encoding.
