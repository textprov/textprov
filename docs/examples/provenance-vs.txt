TextProv selector encoding demo
=================================

Only the SAMPLE line below is provenance-marked. This explanatory text is ordinary Unicode.

Stored form: each non-whitespace grapheme in SAMPLE is followed by U+E0101.

Expected display:
- Without a P+ font: SAMPLE remains readable as plain text; no sawtooth is shown.
- With a P+ font and a shaper that honors the format-14 mapping: SAMPLE is readable with a sawtooth underline.
- In Zed on macOS: CoreText renders plain base glyphs. Zed may also show spacing and green invisible-character marks if it separates selectors from their bases.
- In other editors: installing the font is necessary but not sufficient; the text shaper must honor the mapping.
