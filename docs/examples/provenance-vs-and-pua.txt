Expected display:
- Without a P+ font: VS SAMPLE remains readable without a sawtooth; PUA SAMPLE normally shows missing-glyph boxes.
- With a P+ font and a shaper that honors format-14 mappings: both samples can appear as readable text with a sawtooth underline.
- With CoreText on macOS: VS SAMPLE renders as plain base glyphs. Direct tests selected the PUA glyphs, but each editor path must be checked separately.
- In Zed on macOS: VS SAMPLE may also show spacing and green invisible-character marks if highlighting separates selectors from their bases.
